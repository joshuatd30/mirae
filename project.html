Model Definition

DESEI is not a single-product export; it is an operationally reproducible deployment model.

A model that integrates Japanese technologies to form decentralized environmental and sanitation infrastructure in Southeast Asian island communities.

It is not stand-alone product export, but a replicable deployment model built on clearly defined operating conditions.

Key Operating Conditions
  • Water is drawn manually from storage.
  • Water is transported ~100m to City Hall.
  • A water pressure of 0.4 MPa is supplied from an auxiliary supply tank, enabling a stable supply of UF60.
  • Output is pH 12.5 alkaline e-WASH.

Technical Conditions
  • UF60 requires constant water pressure
  • Therefore, after ground transportation, it is transferred to an auxiliary supply tank
  • Stable supply to the processing line with an electric pump of 0.4 Mpa
Why this matters
  • Quality output with lower power dependency.
  • Links decentralized access with centralized treatment.
  • Makes deployment conditions replicable.
