How to use photocatalytic Super Sol and sewage filter design in Super Sol estero

In order to improve the current situation of Estero, as an effective measure, we would like to propose to improve sewage by using an artificial porous lightweight foam material (Super Sol) made from crushed, calcined, and foamed waste glass, which has been proven in Japan, as a filter. In addition, last year, a new Super Sol with a titanium dioxide photocatalyst was developed.

  • I would like to propose a design proposal that uses ordinary Super Sol as a sewage filter to promote the purification of sewage, and as a final step, a photocatalyst Super Sol is placed at the outlet to the sea to attack E. coli.
  • Initial trials of the photocatalytic Super Sol (Manila) showed a certain effect (BOD decreased from 12 mg/L to 8 mg/L), but validation experiments are needed to obtain accurate effects under actual local conditions.

Estero's Super Sol Purification System Design

By using e-WASH, you can expect to reduce the use of synthetic detergents by two-thirds and further inhibit the growth of organic matter.

We will create a mechanism for the reservoir to maximize contact between the photocatalyst Super Sol and the water surface.

Specifically,

  • Install a photocatalyst Super Sol wrapped in a net to match the width of the estero.
  • The size of the net should be designed with maintainability and safety in mind.
  • The installation method is to erect supports at the four corners and connect the photocatalyst Super Sol so that it can be moved.
  • The photocatalyst Super Sol is an L2 type, so it has the characteristic of floating on the surface of the water. Constantly floating on the surface of the water and in contact with the water of the estero.
